Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

The short version TrueLevel collects nothing. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Every reading stays on your device.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the TrueLevel iOS app (the "App"), published by Ilia Filiaev (the "Developer"), handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The App does not have user accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not communicate with any server controlled by the Developer or any third party.

Data the App reads on your device

  • Device motion. While the App is open and in the foreground, it reads the gravity vector from Apple's CoreMotion framework to compute tilt. This data is processed in memory and is never transmitted.
  • Saved angles. When you capture a reading, its pitch, roll, inclination, optional label, and timestamp are stored on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions the App may request

  • Motion & Fitness — required to compute tilt from device motion.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, there is nothing to access, export, or delete. You can remove all on-device data by deleting the App from your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in the App release notes.
